How to Arrange a Replacement Shipment

Oops! A mix-up happened — but don’t worry, we can fix it fast! Follow these steps:

  1. Find the order : Ask the customer for their order number (and double-check the item they expected).
  2. Create a replacement : In the order system, select "Replacement Order" and confirm the correct item and size/color.
  3. Arrange the return : Generate a prepaid return label and email it to the customer with easy drop-off instructions.
  4. Confirm delivery : Share the new tracking number and expected delivery date with the customer.
  5. Reassure them : Let them know we’re taking care of everything and follow up with a confirmation email.
Happy customers are returning customers — let’s make it right with a smile!
