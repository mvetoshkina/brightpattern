Boiler Cover — Cancellation Policy

Home Protection Policy Procedures Article type: Procedure

When to use

When a customer requests cancellation of an active boiler protection plan.

Cancellation process

  1. Verify the account holder's identity.
  2. Establish the reason for cancellation.
  3. For price-related requests, check eligibility for alternative plans.
  4. If the customer declines, continue with cancellation.
  5. Review the account's cancellation terms, including notice requirements and charges.
  6. Explain the effective cancellation date and arrange written confirmation.

Illustrative policy conditions

Cooling-off period

If the plan is within its cooling-off period, follow the cooling-off cancellation procedure.

Outside cooling-off period

Review the contract's notice period, possible charges and refund eligibility before confirming cancellation.
